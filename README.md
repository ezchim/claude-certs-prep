# Claude Certs Prep — Read · Listen · Train

Independent, open study packs for the four Anthropic Claude certifications,
built for three modes of study:

- **Read** — full study notes in Markdown, EPUB, and PDF (this repo), or in the
  web reader online.
- **Listen** — every pack as a narrated audiobook with sentence-level
  highlighting, on the site.
- **Train** — a practice dojo with exam-style questions, on the site.

**Start here → [claude-certs.aicourse.page](https://claude-certs.aicourse.page/)** —
the complete experience (reading, audio, practice) lives on the site. This
repo holds the reading material and e-book exports.

## Languages and accessibility

Every pack ships in four written editions:

- **English** — the canonical edition.
- **ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English** (`*-ste.md`) — controlled
  vocabulary, short sentences, one meaning per word. Written for non-native
  English readers and anyone who finds dense technical prose a barrier:
  same content, lower reading load.
- **繁體中文** (`zh-hant/`) and **简体中文** (`zh-hans/`) — full translations.

On the site, audio is additionally available in Mandarin (TW/CN) and
Cantonese (粵語).

## What's in this repo

- `*/` — the five study packs as Markdown (English + STE).
- `zh-hans/`, `zh-hant/` — the Chinese editions.
- `exports/` — EPUB and PDF builds of every pack, incl. Kindle-friendly PDFs
  and Chinese EPUBs.

Audio is intentionally not in this repo (file sizes) — it streams free from
the site.

## Independence and sources

These are **original study notes**, not official Anthropic material, and this
project is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic**.
No official course lessons, quizzes, or partner materials are reproduced.
Content is grounded in public sources: Anthropic's public documentation, the
public Claude Academy course outlines, and the official Exam Guide v1.0
(July 2026) for each certification — download the guides from the official
[Pearson VUE Anthropic program page](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html).

Product names, model IDs, and exam mechanics change. Always verify against
[support.claude.com](https://support.claude.com),
[docs.anthropic.com](https://docs.anthropic.com), and
[academy.claude.com](https://academy.claude.com) before exam day.
